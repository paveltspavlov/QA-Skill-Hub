# Architecture Decision Helper

## Procedure
1. Extract the decision, goals, non-goals, constraints, current system, and decision deadline.
2. List viable options, including keeping the current design. Do not invent requirements or benchmarks.
3. Compare on the relevant criteria: complexity, operability, security, cost drivers, performance, migration, and reversibility.
4. State assumptions, trade-offs, risks, and what evidence would change the recommendation.
5. Draft a concise ADR with context, decision, consequences, alternatives, and review date.

## Output
A comparison table and ADR-ready decision record.
