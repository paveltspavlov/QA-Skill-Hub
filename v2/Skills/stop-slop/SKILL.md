# Stop Slop

## Procedure
1. Identify audience, purpose, tone, required terminology, claims, and citations.
2. Remove redundant framing, filler, generic conclusions, inflated adjectives, repeated transitions, and needless lists.
3. Prefer concrete nouns and verbs, active voice, varied sentence rhythm, and specific evidence already in the text.
4. Preserve meaning, caveats, citations, technical terms, and author voice; do not invent examples or facts.
5. Return the revision and flag unclear passages instead of smoothing them over.

## Output
A clean revised passage plus unresolved ambiguity that prevents faithful editing.
